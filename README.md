## 📖 Overview

**Departure at 4: Grekhov Express** (internal title: *Working*) is a mystery visual novel made with [Ren'Py](https://www.renpy.org/).

Players step into the shoes of **Louis**, who finds himself aboard the luxurious and mysterious Grekhov Express with fragmented memories. As you navigate through the train's carriages, interact with peculiar passengers, and uncover hidden clues, a sinister plot unfolds. When tragedy strikes and time itself loops, you must re-examine the evidence, uncover motives, and find the truth before it's too late.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **Interactive Carriage Maps:** Explore multiple train carriages (Carriages 1 through 5 and beyond) using interactive point-and-click imagemaps.
- **Branching Dialogue & Choices:** Engage with a diverse ensemble of characters. Your decisions influence interactions and trackable story choices.
- **Time Loop & Mystery Investigation:** Experience a shifting narrative where carriage conditions change, secrets are revealed, and crime scenes must be investigated.
- **Mahjong Minigame:** Test your skills with an integrated Mahjong tile-matching minigame during your journey.
- **Achievements System:** Unlock in-game achievements as you explore, discover hidden secrets, and progress through the story.
- **Custom UI & Customization:** Includes customizable dialogue font sizes, screen shake toggles, audio settings, and custom visual themes.

## 👥 Characters

- **Louis:** The protagonist searching for answers after finding himself unexpectedly on the express.
- **Dorothy:** The train conductor welcoming passengers aboard.
- **Father Matthew:** A devout passenger offering prayers and warnings of disrupted holiness.
- **Fedora:** An ambitious reporter seeking the ultimate scoop and story on the Grekhov Express.
- **Eduard:** The carriage bartender who knows everyone's tab and habits.
- **Jackie:** Staff member trying to keep the train clean and orderly.
- **Shunyu:** An enthusiastic gambler who loves high-stakes Mahjong games.
- **Kimmy & Himmel:** A wealthy high-society couple traveling in the luxury cabin.
- **Sebastian & Misha:** A protective butler overseeing young Misha.
- **Agatha:** The pragmatic train medical staff member.
- **Ichora:** The train engineer who provides key assistance.
- **Pattison:** A cryptic mail worker handling letters on the train.
- **Alexander:** A quirky merchant offering rare items like banned maple sugar.

## 🎮 How to Play & Controls

### Controls
| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **Left Click / Space / Enter** | Advance dialogue / Select choices / Interact with hotspots |
| **Escape / Game Menu** | Open Settings, Save/Load, and Game Menu |
| **Ctrl (Hold)** | Fast forward / Skip dialogue |
| **Tab** | Toggle dialogue skipping |
| **Mouse Wheel** | Scroll back / History backlog |

## 🚀 Running the Game

### Requirements
- **Ren'Py SDK** (v8.0 or later recommended) or a pre-built distribution of the game.

### Launching via Ren'Py SDK
1. Download and install the [Ren'Py SDK](https://www.renpy.org/latest.html).
2. Open the Ren'Py Launcher.
3. Select **"Add Existing Project"** and point to this project folder.
4. Click **"Launch Project"**.
